# BEYONDPROOF 🛡️
### The Universal Digital Trust & Forensic Verification Platform

**Beyond Trust. Beyond Doubt.**

BEYONDPROOF is an AI-powered digital trust and forensic verification platform designed to investigate the authenticity and integrity of digital information.

In an era where AI-generated content, deepfakes, forged documents, manipulated media, and digital identity fraud are becoming increasingly sophisticated, BEYONDPROOF aims to provide a unified platform for analysing, verifying, and investigating digital evidence.

Unlike conventional verification tools that focus on a single type of content, BEYONDPROOF brings multiple forensic capabilities together into one intelligent ecosystem.

### 🚀 Core Engines

1. **Identity Guardian** – Identity document analysis, face similarity checks, and identity fraud indicators.
2. **Document Verifier** – Reference-free document forensic analysis, OCR, metadata examination, and tampering detection.
3. **Media Truth Engine (AFTERIMAGE)** – AI-powered image, video, and audio manipulation analysis.
4. **Source Tracker** – SHA-256 fingerprinting, file integrity verification, and digital provenance tracking.
5. **Trust Graph** – Connects identities, documents, sources, and digital evidence through relationship visualisation.
6. **AI Risk Analyzer** – Combines forensic findings into explainable risk assessments.
7. **Verification Hub** – A unified dashboard for verification history, forensic findings, and downloadable reports.

### ⚙️ Key Capabilities

- Reference-free digital document investigation.
- AI-assisted image and deepfake analysis.
- Video and audio forensic analysis.
- OCR-based document intelligence.
- Metadata and structural inconsistency detection.
- SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprinting.
- Digital provenance investigation.
- Evidence relationship visualisation.
- Explainable risk scoring.
- Comprehensive verification reports.

### 🧰 Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, Node.js
- **AI/ML:** PyTorch, OpenCV, Hugging Face
- **Database:** PostgreSQL, Redis
- **Forensics:** PaddleOCR, FFmpeg, Librosa
- **Security:** JWT, SHA-256, cryptographic verification

### 🎯 Vision

Our vision is to build a unified digital trust infrastructure that helps individuals, institutions, organisations, and investigators make more informed decisions about the digital information they encounter.

BEYONDPROOF does not simply aim to detect fake content. It aims to explain suspicious indicators, examine available evidence, and establish greater transparency in digital verification.
